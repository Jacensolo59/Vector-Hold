ENEMIES

Four geometric ships. Round 1 uses the written base. Each later round adds one step,
so "base + per round" means base + per * (round - 1). Percentage stats are the
number of percent, so 1 means +1% of the base each round after the first:

  value = base * (1 + percent / 100 * (round - 1))

The number under each ship is its defense this round. Round 1 uses the base.
Each later round adds the listed percent of that base. A circle is 10 on round 1
and 10.9 on round 10.
Forward speed is how fast they come down. Side speed is how fast they weave.
Each one keeps its own lane. The hull is the full bottom edge, just above the weapon tray.
The approach is long enough to shoot, and short enough that a round is a fight
rather than a minute of empty space.

HULL CURVE
Round 1 uses the written hull. Each later round also multiplies hull by
enemy.hp_curve. The first enemy.hp_grace rounds stay close to the written
stats. After that, enemy.hp_bend lifts the exponent. Attack uses enemy.atk_curve
with no bend. Defense, the number under each ship, uses enemy.def_curve the
same way as hull, with its own grace and bend.

A match level is only a small damage bump. It will not carry a run.
With no research the opening is comfortable, the round-10 boss is a spike,
and the line breaks in the mid to high teens. In-match cards push that a
little. Research is what clears rounds this account could not clear before.
The bend still beats a fully researched ship if the run goes deep, so the
next deploy is always trying for a higher best round.

HEX BOSS
  One hexagon comes down the center on every 10th round (10, 20, 30...).
  Hull 420 + 18 per round, then the shared hull curve and the boss-round bonus.
  Defense 80, then the defense curve. Slower than the others.
  Attack 30, then the attack curve. Melee.
  Each boss round also adds 10% hull to every enemy, including the boss.
  That bonus stacks and lasts until the match ends. Round 10 is +10%.
  Round 20 is +20%. A new match clears it.
  The first boss is a spike you can survive. Later bosses are the run's wall.

CIRCLE  basic
  Hull 10 + 2.4 per round
  Defense 10, then the defense curve
  Forward 240
  Side 145
  Attack 9, then the attack curve
  Range melee

SQUARE  tank
  Hull 48 + 6.5 per round
  Defense 28, then the defense curve
  Forward 155
  Side 95
  Attack 12, then the attack curve
  Range melee

TRIANGLE  fast
  Hull 14 + 1.3 per round
  Defense 5, then the defense curve
  Forward 420
  Side 250
  Attack 15, then the attack curve
  Range melee

RECTANGLE  ranged
  Hull 26 + 3.2 per round
  Defense 14, then the defense curve
  Forward 250
  Side 150
  Attack 16, then the attack curve
  Range 1400. They stop and shoot. The railgun, laser, and tesla
  prefer whoever will hurt the hull soonest, so a shooter is not ignored
  just because a circle is closer.

Colors are only so you can tell them apart: cyan circles, amber squares, red triangles,
violet rectangles, gold hexes, green diamonds, orange chevrons, white crosses, yellow mines.

DIAMOND  splitter, from round 3
  Slow. On death it breaks into two small circles that keep coming from where it died.
  Those pieces have 45% of a circle's hull, attack, gold, and XP, and they do not split.

CHEVRON  guard, from round 6, only after Tesla is unlocked
  It does not appear in a match until the account has unlocked the tesla.
  The first hit each second is ignored, unless the ship is stunned.
  A tesla stun opens it. Conductive then makes the hits that land hurt more.

CROSS  support, from round 7
  It does not attack. It moves onto the ship with the most hull left and restores
  6% of that ship's max hull each second. Kill it or the tank and the hex boss outlast the round.
  A stun freezes the healing.

MINE  timed hull hit, from round 8
  It does not shoot and it does not ram. Partway down the lane it arms.
  1.35 seconds later the hull takes the hit, even if you killed the mine after it armed.
  Killing it before it arms stops the blast. You get no gold if it goes off on its own.

ROUND TYPES
  Convoy, every 3rd round that is not a boss or an escort. The wave spawns in a
  tight pack in the middle of the lane.
  Escort, the round before each boss. Most of the wave is rectangles, plus two extra.
  Marked, one ship each round from round 6. An amber ring, double hull, double gold and XP.
  The hex boss is never the marked ship.

Wave mix is a design fill. The document lists the ships but not how many spawn.
Defaults in balance_data.gd under waves.* :
  Circles from round 1, 5 + 0.9 per round after.
  Triangles from round 2.
  Rectangles from round 4.
  Squares from round 5.
Spawn spacing starts at 0.72s and tightens slowly, never faster than spawn_interval_min.
Alive ships are capped by waves.max_alive so a typo in the console cannot freeze the tab.

The DEV console can spawn one of each type into the current round immediately.
