VECTOR HOLD
Tower defense, brought back to basic. No ads.

You hold a line in deep space. Ships only come from in front of you.
Your railgun, and later the weapons you arm, fire on their own.
You do not steer and you cannot shoot behind the hull line.

A run:
1. Deploy from the main screen.
2. Survive rounds. Killing ships grants match XP and gold.
3. Every even match level, pick 1 of 3 upgrades. The run pauses.
4. If the hull hits zero, the run ends. Gold already earned stays.
5. Spend gold in Research. That progress stays on this device.
   Damage ranks are the long game. One run buys a little. A deep run buys one
   expensive rank. There is always a next purchase.
6. Act level, from lifetime XP, unlocks weapons slowly, then keeps adding a
   little hull and defense to every future run. Each act is one stretch of
   the trip home, through their territory. The line is under the act bar.

Where the code lives:
- scripts/formulas.gd        pure math, safe to retune and test
- scripts/balance_data.gd    every default number and the dev-console rows
- scripts/balance.gd         defaults plus your saved overrides
- scripts/profile.gd         gold, act level, research
- scripts/battle.gd          the match: spawns, weapons, damage, UI
- scripts/field_view.gd      line-art drawing only
- scripts/dev_console.gd     the balance console
- scripts/main.gd            menu, research, settings, these notes
- scripts/shapes.gd          geometric ship and enemy outlines
- scripts/sfx.gd             synthesized beeps, no audio files
- scripts/self_test.gd       checks the formulas against the design doc

Read the other notes in this folder for enemies, weapons, upgrades, research, and the console.
The same files are the Field Notes screen inside the game.
