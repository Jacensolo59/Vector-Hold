BATTLEFIELD AND RULES

The live field is 12000 long by 1000 wide.
The document length is 3000, with weapons capped at 2750.
The live lane is four times that, and every weapon range is scaled by the same
four, so a railgun still reaches most of the approach. Ships spawn at the top
and take the full length to reach the hull.
The whole lane is drawn as open space. There is no gridded panel.
Stars drift down the view. A faint amber line is the hull, the full width
of the field, just above the weapon tray.
On screen the ship is the bottom center of the battle view, pointing up.
Enemies spawn above it and travel down. World Y grows toward the ship.

Hull and defense
- A match starts at 100 hull and 5 defense.
- The document says leveling raises the character's base stats and the weapons.
  Weapon stats still use the "+ per level" formulas, but the per-level
  amounts are small. A match level will not outrun the round curve.
  Hull gains +1 per match level and defense +0.08 per level.
  Research, not the match level, is what pushes a later run past today's best round.

Damage against defense
  dealt = raw * (100 / (100 + defense))
  Defense penetration and laser shred reduce defense before that division.
  At 0 defense, damage is full. At 100 defense, damage is halved.

Contact
- The player's hit line is the full width of the field, just above the weapon tray.
  Circles, squares, triangles, diamonds, and chevrons ram when they reach that line.
  Mines do not ram. They arm partway down the lane and the blast hits the hull on a fuse.
  Crosses do not ram or shoot. They stick to the fullest ship and heal it.
  They do not home on the ship, except the cross. Each other one keeps a lane and weaves.
- Rectangles stop at their weapon range and shoot on a timer.
  The document does not give that timer. The default is enemy.fire_interval = 2.2 seconds.

Targeting
- Railgun, laser, and tesla pick the ship that will hurt the hull soonest.
  A shooter already in range outranks a rammer that is still far away.
  A cross outranks ordinary ships, because it is keeping a tank alive.
  A mine is scored by how soon it will arm, not by when it would reach the hull.
  Once a mine is armed, killing it does not stop the blast, so guns leave it.
  A marked ship is preferred by that same score times its hull multiplier.
- Missiles still prefer the highest hull in range.
- The laser is sticky. It waits laser.retarget seconds before it will pick a new ship.

Match level cap is 100. XP needed to leave a level is xp.match_base + xp.match_per_level * level.
Kill XP and gold amounts are not in the document. They live on each enemy as *_xp and *_gold.

Rounds do not end the game. Clear a wave, wait waves.between seconds, and the next round starts.
The run ends only when the hull breaks.
