RESEARCH AND ACT LEVEL

Gold is earned on each kill and saved immediately, even if the hull breaks
or you abandon the run. Spend it from the Research button on the main screen.
There is no shop inside a match.

Act XP uses the same kill XP values as the match, added to a lifetime total.
The main screen, the battle HUD, pause, and the defeat screen show how far
the current act has filled. Act level starts at 1.
XP to leave an act = xp.act_base + xp.act_per_level * act level.
That curve is long on purpose. Missiles are a few sessions in, not a few minutes.
The last weapon is a milestone, not the end of the game. Act level keeps climbing
after every weapon is unlocked.

THE WAY HOME
Each act is one stretch of the same trip: through their territory, toward home.
The line is on the main screen, under the act bar, and again when you pause or the hull breaks.
  1 Outer Dark       railgun only. You are pointed home and still in their sky.
  2 The Picket       missiles, taken off the first patrol line.
  3 The Belt         the laser, cut from a dead hull.
  4 Charged Space    the tesla, rigged from the storms. Their guards answer.
  5 The Wing         two drones fall back into your formation.
  6 Inner Burn       the flamethrower, for ships that get inside the guns.
  7 The Approaches   every weapon is online. Their beacons are still firing.
  8 Harbor Light     home is on the scope. The last stretch does not open itself.
After that the acts rotate three closing lines: The Door, The Signal, Last Lane.
You can see it. They still hold the space in front of it.

Act level does not raise weapon damage. That is research.
Match level, the bar inside a run, is only a small damage bump. It will not
outrun the enemies. Each act after the first does add hull and defense to every future run
(player.hull_per_act, player.defense_per_act). That mastery is why a hundredth
hour still changes the next deploy.

DAMAGE RESEARCH
Each weapon has a damage multiplier track.
  +2% damage per rank
  cap 80 ranks
  first rank costs 350 gold
  each rank the price rises 6.2%
  every 10 ranks, that rise itself grows by another 1.4%
A fresh run pays for a few ranks. Deep runs pay for one expensive rank.
Buying the cheapest rank across weapons stays useful for a very long time.
Finishing every track is not the expected evening.

  cost starts at research.damage_start
  for each rank already owned:
      band = owned_rank / 10
      cost = cost * (1 + damage_inc + damage_band_inc * band)
  the price you pay is that cost, rounded up

RAILGUN PENETRATION
Separate from railgun damage.
  +0.5% defense penetration per rank (the fraction 0.005)
  Cap 40, which is 20% of enemy defense ignored
  Starts at 600 gold and climbs faster than damage research
  This is the answer to squares and the hex boss, not a dump stat.

MISSILE EDGE
  Edge damage starts at 25% of the center hit.
  Each rank adds 0.5 percentage points (0.005).
  At rank 60 the edge is 55%.
  Price uses the same gold curve as damage research.

LASER, TESLA, DRONES, FLAMETHROWER
The document leaves their research sections blank aside from the shared damage track.
Only that shared +2% track is implemented for them.

A weapon's research stays locked until the matching act unlock, except the railgun.
Buying is disabled when you cannot afford it or the track is capped.
Reset Progress in Settings erases gold, act XP, research, and best round on this device.
Balance overrides are separate and are cleared from the DEV console's RESET button.
