CONTROLS ON MOBILE AND PC

There is no movement. The ship holds the line.
Touch and mouse both press the same buttons. Targets are at least 44 pixels tall.

Main screen
  The name sits at the top. The version is the small line under it.
  A box at the top left holds the act bar, gold, and best round.
  DEPLOY, RESEARCH, and FIELD NOTES are the smaller buttons under the ship.
  DEV and SET sit at the right of that top row. SET is volume, shake, and erase.

During a run
  The field fills the screen down to the weapon tray.
  Hull, round, gold, match XP, and act XP sit in a clear box at the top left.
  That box is only a readout. It does not cover the field as its own row.
  SET or Esc    pause. The button stays at the top right. Resume, read notes, or abandon.
                Abandon keeps gold already banked.
  HIDE WEAPONS  slides the weapon tray down so the field gets taller.
                The ship icon and weapon glyphs sit on that tray, one side each,
                and move down with it. That is the layout from the design document.
  Upgrade cards appear on their own. You cannot dismiss them without a pick.

Keyboard
  F1 or `       balance console
  Esc           pause or resume
  Enter / Space activate the focused button

Sound is synthesized and starts after the first tap or click, which mobile
requires. Railgun is a crack, missiles a whoosh, laser a clean pulse, tesla
a buzz with pops, the flamethrower a roar, and drones a short chirp.
The volume slider is in Settings, and again on the pause screen during a run.
Minus and plus step it by 5%. 0% mutes. Turn screen shake off there if you prefer a still camera.

The layout scales with the resolution. 1280px wide is 1×. A 1920-wide monitor
draws at 1.5× and a 3840-wide monitor at 3×. The ship, enemies, shots, drones,
weapon icons, and menu type all follow that scale. A window under 960px wide
stays 1:1 so a phone does not shrink the text away.

The battlefield is 1000 wide by 3000 long. The lane is mapped onto the battle
view, and the ships are sized from the view width so they stay readable at
every resolution in the settings list.

The web build is the Godot game exported for HTML5 with threads off,
so it runs inside a normal browser on a phone or a desktop without special headers.
The Godot project source is the game. Open project.godot in Godot 4.7 if you
want a native mobile or desktop export later.
