WEAPONS

You start a match with the railgun only. Other weapons are offered as upgrade cards
once your act level has unlocked them. Order follows the design document:

  Act 1  Railgun, always
  Act 2  Missiles
  Act 3  Laser
  Act 4  Tesla
  Act 5  Drones
  Act 6  Flamethrower

Change unlock.* if you want them earlier.

All owned weapons scale with the current match level:
  stat = base + per_level * (level - 1)
  fire rate = base * (1 + percent_per_level * (level - 1))
Level 1 is the written base. Research damage multipliers and in-match upgrade ranks stack on top.

RAILGUN
  Damage 24 + 3 per level
  Fire rate 1.20 per second, +1.6% per level
  Range 11000 on the live 12000 field
  It aims from the muzzle at where that ship will be when the bolt arrives,
  then the center bolt keeps that lead while it is in flight. Pierce and static
  are upgrade cards, not the base gun.

MISSILES
  Damage 80 + 6 per level
  Reload 4 seconds
  Range 11000 on the live 12000 field (document 2750 on a 3000 field)
  They home, then explode. Damage falls off from 100% at the center to the edge fraction
  (25% until missile-edge research raises it).
  "Good against tanks" is why they aim at the highest hull in range.

LASER
  Damage 22 + 2.2 per level, applied every 0.25 seconds while a target is held
  Range 10000 on the live field (document 2500)
  It keeps firing until that ship dies or leaves range, then waits retarget seconds.
  Over 1 second of focus it removes 40% of the target's defense (laser.shred_cap).
  Shred fades if the beam moves on.

TESLA
  Damage 16 + 1.6 per level
  Fire rate 1.15 per second, +1.2% per level
  Range 7000 on the live field (document 1750)
  Hits 3 ships before upgrades. The first is stunned 1.6s.
  Each later jump loses 0.20s of stun, never below stun_min.
  A jump only happens if the next ship is within bounce_link (2800) and still in weapon range.

DRONES
  Start with 2. The document says fast, medium hull, medium defense, low damage.
  Hull 45 and defense 12 are fills (drone.hp, drone.defense).
  Damage 6 + 2.2 per level, per drone
  Fire rate 1.15 per second, +2% per level, per drone
  Detection range 8000, measured from the player like the other weapons.
  Attack range 1000, measured from the drone. They only shoot ships in front of them.
  If nothing is inside the detection range, they return to orbiting the player.
  Overlapping an enemy still chews their hull. Downed drones relaunch from the ship after drone.respawn seconds.
  The missile upgrade is separate. See the upgrades note.

FLAMETHROWER
  Damage 4 + 1.2 per level every 0.25 seconds to every ship in the cone
  Range starts at 1000 and the upgrade adds 1000, capped at 4000
  (document 250, plus 250, cap 1000)
  The base cone angle is flame.half_angle (0.30 radians). That angle is a fill.
  It only reaches forward.

Overclock, an extra card when the upgrade pool is thin, speeds every weapon's timer.
It does not raise damage.
